# Beyond Tees: Product Partners

Rule: every product is printed on demand or stocked once at the fulfillment partner, so nothing needs hand packing.

## Resident trading cards
| Use | Partner | How it works | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free card in every order** (launch) | **Printful pack-ins** | Print the 6 resident cards in bulk (any card printer), mail stock to Printful, and Printful adds one to every order automatically. | Printful can't choose a card per order, so it's a random resident, which works as a collectible. Orders from a facility with no card stock ship without one. Small per-order fee plus monthly storage. Check current limits and fees in the dashboard. |
| **Sellable card packs** (phase 2) | **QPMN** (QP Market Network) | Print-on-demand cards and booster packs from one unit, white-label shipping, API for custom stores, randomized rare cards across packs, holo foil. | Claims come from QPMN's own pages. Order samples and read the API docs before committing. Card packs ship separately from tees. |

**Card idea:** each card shows a resident, their "stats" (Pun Power, Torque, Sass, Range), and a QR code to their animated short. Rare holo versions go in sellable packs only.

## Other car-native products
| Product | Partner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stickers and bumper stickers | Printful or Printify | Easy add-on with the best margin. Use for "I brake for snacks" style lines. |
| Hats, hoodies, mugs | Printful | Same print files (see PRINT-SPECS.md for the 4500×5400 master; hats use embroidery files). |
| Car accessories (plate frames, seat covers and more) | Printify | Printify lists 1,300+ car accessory products. Pick one print provider per product. |
| **Car air fresheners** | PrintKK, Odemly, Printsimple (EU) | The most on-brand add-on ("Smells like Grandma's wagon"). Odemly is a Shopify app, so it won't plug into our store without an API; check PrintKK for an API first. No air freshener found on Printify. |

## Recommendation
1. Launch: tees plus stickers on Printful, with resident cards as a Printful pack-in.
2. Next: air fresheners (after checking an API and getting samples), then hats and hoodies for fall.
3. Then: sellable holo card packs through QPMN, tied to the Mileage Club (500 miles unlocks a rare card).
