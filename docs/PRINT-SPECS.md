# Coopteeville Print File Spec (for designers)

The goal: sharp, vivid prints that look the same at Printful and Printify. Follow this exactly and run the checker before handing anything off.

```bash
npm run check:print -- path/to/cv-013_front_light.png
```

---

## 1. The master file (one size for both vendors)

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Canvas** | **4500 × 5400 px** (portrait) | Equals 15 × 18 in at 300 DPI, Printify's full-front target and Printful's standard DTG print area. Both vendors scale *down* cleanly. Scaling *up* blurs the print. |
| **Resolution** | **300 DPI** (set in the export) | Printful accepts 150–300; above 300 adds nothing. 300 is our house rule. |
| **Format** | **PNG, 8-bit RGBA (PNG-24 with alpha)** | DTG needs a transparent background. No JPG, no PDF, no flattened white. |
| **Background** | **100% transparent** | Any white or colored box behind the art gets printed as a box. |
| **Color profile** | **sRGB IEC61966-2.1**, embedded | Both vendors print from RGB. Do not send CMYK. |
| **Max file size** | Under 200 MB (aim for under 30 MB) | Big empty canvases compress small. |

**Never upscale.** If a file started small (AI output, a screenshot), rebuild it as vector. Don't stretch it to 4500 px.

## 2. Where the art sits on the canvas

The canvas is the *maximum* print area. Most of our designs are smaller and sit **top-centered**.

| Placement | Artwork size on the 4500 × 5400 canvas | Printed size | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard front (default)** | ~3300–3600 px wide, top edge at 0–150 px | 11–12 in wide | Most saying tees |
| **Full front** | Up to 4500 × 5400 px | Up to 15 × 18 in | Big character art |
| **Left chest** | Separate canvas **1200 × 1200 px** | 4 × 4 in | Small Coop logo, "resident" badge |
| **Back** | Separate canvas **4500 × 5400 px** | Up to 15 × 18 in | Club Kits, back hits |
| **Plate Maker tee** | Plate graphic 3000 px wide, top-centered | 10 in wide | Generated by the store, same rules |

Rules:
- **Center horizontally to the pixel.** Off-center by 50 px is visible on a chest.
- **Keep the top of the art at the top of the canvas.** The vendor places the canvas top about 3 in below the collar. Empty space at the top pushes the design down onto the belly.
- **Keep text 150 px (½ in) in from all canvas edges.** Vendors can shift prints up to about ½ in.
- **Shirt sizes:** vendors scale the same file for S–3XL (on some products, small sizes get a reduced area). Check the mockup at size S and size 3XL before approving.

## 3. Line, text, and detail minimums (house rules)

Detail smaller than this fills in or breaks on fabric. These are conservative house rules, so prove them with a sample order.

| Element | Minimum at 300 DPI |
|---|---|
| Line or stroke weight | **6 px** (≈ 0.5 mm / 1.5 pt); **9 px on dark shirts** |
| Smallest text cap height | **75 px** (¼ in). Tagline and signature text included |
| Gap between shapes (knockouts) | **6 px** |
| Body text (sayings) | Cap height 250 px or more (about ⅞ in). It has to read from across a room |

Our display face, **Bungee**, is chunky on purpose. Don't swap in thin script fonts for small text.

## 4. Transparency, shadows, and gradients (the #1 cause of bad prints)

DTG on dark shirts prints a white base layer under the color. Semi-transparent pixels get a patchy base layer, which shows up as a rough, dirty halo.

- **No soft drop shadows, glows, feathered edges, or fade-to-transparent gradients.**
- **Every pixel should be either fully opaque or fully transparent.** Anti-aliased edges are fine; large soft areas are not.
- Need a shadow or texture? Convert it to **halftone dots** (solid dots of varying size, 35–45 lines per inch) so every dot is 100% opaque.
- Gradients are fine *inside* opaque shapes (color to color), never color to transparent.
- The checker flags files where more than 2% of the art's pixels are semi-transparent.

## 5. Color

- Design in **sRGB**. Use our brand colors from `docs/BRAND.md` as a starting point.
- **DTG can't print neon, fluorescent, or metallic ink.** Very bright screen colors (pure `#00FF00`, electric blue) print duller. Ask for a sample before approving bright greens or blues.
- **Light shirts vs. dark shirts get separate files.**
  - `_light` file: dark ink outlines (`#1C2033`) are fine.
  - `_dark` file: swap dark outlines for white or cream, or knock them out so the shirt color shows through. **Never print black ink on a black shirt.** It wastes ink and looks muddy.
- Don't print a color that matches the shirt (heather-gray art on a heather shirt disappears).
- Avoid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) blocks larger than about 3 × 3 in on dark shirts. Big solid areas feel stiff ("plasticky"); add halftone texture to break them up.

## 6. Source files and naming

Every design is delivered as a folder:

```
cv-013_honk-if-you-love-naps/
  cv-013_front_light.png    # 4500×5400, for white / heather / sky shirts
  cv-013_front_dark.png     # 4500×5400, for black / cherry shirts
  cv-013_source.svg         # or .ai / .afdesign; vector, text OUTLINED
  cv-013_mockup.png         # vendor mockup on each shirt color, size M
```

- Build in **vector** (Illustrator, Affinity Designer, Figma, Inkscape) and export to PNG at the final size.
- **Outline all text** in the source file so fonts can't swap.
- Print files are stored in R2 at `print/<id>_front_<light|dark>.png`, and the store's catalog points to them.

## 7. Vendor-specific notes

**Printful**
- Open the product, then the **File guidelines** tab. The print area differs by product and some sizes. If it shows 12 × 16 in, the 4500 × 5400 master still works because Printful scales it down. Check the mockup.
- Use the **mockup generator** for every shirt color and approve it before publishing.
- Blank: **Bella+Canvas 3001** (soft, consistent). Avoid heavy 100% polyester blanks for DTG.

**Printify**
- Print quality varies by **print provider**. Pick one provider per product and stick with it. Don't let Printify auto-switch providers.
- In Product Creator, confirm the **green "High resolution" indicator** before saving.
- Upload the same 4500 × 5400 master and don't resize it in the editor.

## 8. Pre-flight checklist (designer signs off)

- [ ] 4500 × 5400 px, 300 DPI, RGBA PNG, sRGB
- [ ] Background fully transparent, no stray pixels (checker shows the art box)
- [ ] Art centered horizontally, top-aligned, 150 px margin
- [ ] Lines at least 6 px (9 px for dark), text cap height at least 75 px
- [ ] No soft shadows or glows. Effects are halftoned
- [ ] Separate `_light` and `_dark` files where needed
- [ ] Source file saved with text outlined
- [ ] `npm run check:print` passes
- [ ] Mockups approved at size S and size 3XL
- [ ] **First print of every new design: order one physical sample and wash it once before launch**
