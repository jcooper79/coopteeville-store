To go fully hands-off

1. Map Printful variant IDs and upload print files to R2. Until then, orders fail and land in the exception queue.

2. Ship the AI plate check, then set PLATE_AUTO_APPROVE to true. Plate orders then print without review.

3. Add a retry queue (Cloudflare Queues) so temporary Printful or network errors retry on their own.

4. Keep refunds and misprint judgment calls with people. They are rare and involve money or judgment.
